# YvO to CvH Method Mapping

This document records how the validated `A_YvO_2026` workflow is transferred into
`A_CvH_2026`, which assumptions transfer, which do not, and what must be rerun
after edits.

## CvH Design Summary

- Reference source pipeline: `A_YvO_2026`
- Target project: `A_CvH_2026`
- Raw proteomics input: `00_input/CvH_raw.xlsx`
- Analysis metadata: `00_input/CvH_meta.csv`
- Phenotype/source metadata: `00_input/CRm_meta.csv`
- Tissue reference: `00_input/HPA_skeletal_muscle_annotations.tsv`

### Actual CvH structure inspected on 2026-04-30

- Total subjects: 27
- Total raw samples: 41
- CR creatine subjects: 9 at `T1`, 8 at `T2`
- CR placebo subjects: 8 at `T1`, 6 at `T2`
- Healthy controls: 10 at `T1` only
- Fully paired CR subjects: 14
- CR singleton baseline-only subjects: 3 (`CR15`, `CR23`, `CR383`)
- Healthy post-intervention samples: not available

### Consequences for transfer

- YvO's symmetric 2 x 2 repeated-measures design does **not** transfer directly.
- CvH requires two valid model families:
  - `CRvH`: all subjects, baseline cancer-vs-healthy plus pooled CR training response
  - `CR`: cancer-recovery subjects only, supplement-specific baseline and training contrasts
- Any YvO logic requiring healthy post-intervention samples is invalid for CvH.
- Any YvO logic requiring equal paired structure across all groups must be replaced with a CvH-specific analogue.

## Contrast Mapping

| YvO contrast | YvO meaning | CvH analogue | Directionality notes | Transfer decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Training_Young = Young_Post - Young_Pre` | Young training response | `Training_CRE = CRE_T2 - CRE_T1`, `Training_PLA = PLA_T2 - PLA_T1`, or pooled `Training_CR` | CvH splits one YvO axis into supplement-specific and pooled survivor-response contrasts. | Partial; use the contrast matching the biological question. |
| `Training_Old = Old_Post - Old_Pre` | Older training response | Same as above; no age-stratified CvH analogue | No direct age analogue in CvH. | Not directly transferable. |
| `Aging = Old_Pre - Young_Pre` | Baseline age difference | `Cancer_vs_Healthy = mean(CRE_T1, PLA_T1) - H_T1` | Baseline disease-vs-healthy, not chronological aging. | Analogue only. |
| `Interaction = (Old_Post - Old_Pre) - (Young_Post - Young_Pre)` | Differential training response by age | `Supplement_Interaction = (CRE_T2 - CRE_T1) - (PLA_T2 - PLA_T1)` | Same statistical form, different biology. | Direct statistical transfer; biological interpretation is supplement effect modification. |
| YvO reversal axis: `Aging` vs `Training_Old` | Does training move old baseline toward young baseline? | `Cancer_vs_Healthy` vs `Training_CR` | Valid because baseline disease direction and pooled survivor training direction both exist. | Direct analogue with new biology. |

## Non-Transferred or Restricted Items

- YvO healthy/young post-training comparisons do not transfer because CvH has no healthy `T2`.
- YvO age-group language does not transfer anywhere in CvH figures, tables, or manuscript text.
- YvO phenotype prediction/classifier panels do not transfer as a default CvH figure.
- Any figure relying on a symmetric 4-cell repeated-measures design must be rewritten as either:
  - a `CRvH` baseline-plus-pooled-training analogue, or
  - a `CR` supplement-specific repeated-measures analogue.

## Figure Provenance Notes

### Visual language transferred unchanged where valid

- shared palette-based contrast encoding
- explicit Pi/FDR threshold layering
- panel sizing contracts and deterministic label selection
- shared pathway utilities and enrichment summary structure

### Re-derived for CvH

- all contrast labels
- all group and timepoint labels
- all repeated-measures assumptions
- all healthy-control comparisons
- all model splits (`CRvH` vs `CR`)

### Not transferred

- YvO age-specific biological interpretation
- YvO direct post-training healthy-control contrasts
- YvO classifier framing without separate CvH justification
